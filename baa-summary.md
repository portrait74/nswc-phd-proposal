# NSWC PHD-26-S-0001 BAA Summary

## Basic Information

- **Agency:** Naval Surface Warfare Center Port Hueneme Division (NSWC PHD)
- **Announcement:** Broad Agency Announcement (BAA), NSWC PHD-26-S-0001
- **Full proposal deadline:** 22 June 2026, 1500 PDT
- **White paper deadline:** 17 February 2026
- **Questions deadline:** 16 January 2026
- **Proposal validity:** 365 days from submission
- **Submission:** White papers and questions via email to listed business points of contact
- **NAICS:** 541715 - Research and Development in the Physical, Engineering, and Life Sciences

## Important Positioning Notes

- This is a BAA for basic and applied research and related development, not a procurement for routine technical support services.
- White papers are encouraged but not required; as of the current working date, the white paper deadline has passed, so the working path should focus on full proposal preparation.
- The Government may select all, some, or none of the submitted proposals.
- Proposal development costs are not reimbursed.

## Opportunity Areas

1. **Naval Combat System Readiness**
   - Predictive analytics for enemy action understanding and adaptive planning
   - Simulation-based training using virtual and mixed reality
   - AI agents, tutors, and virtual humans for training effectiveness
   - Predictive data analytics for ship maintenance issues
   - Proactive fault monitoring to improve operational availability and reduce downtime

2. **Hypersonic & Suborbital Research and Validation**
   - Hypersonic test-beds, payload validation, instrumentation, materials, GNC, thermal management, telemetry, and autonomous flight termination

3. **Directed Energy, EW, and Cybersecurity**
   - Directed energy validation, electronic warfare, cyber risk assessment, intrusion detection, post-quantum cryptography, and multi-domain simulation

4. **Manufacturing & Prototyping**
   - Rapid hardware development, environmental qualification testing, advanced manufacturing, embedded systems, and rapid prototyping

5. **Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Capability**
   - UAS validation, flight-worthiness support, telemetry, antenna systems, low detection probability communication, and advanced communication protocols

## Recommended First Proposal Direction

Start with **Topic 2: Hypersonic & Suborbital Research and Validation**.

Rationale:

- David's paper on predictive lateral evasive maneuver guidance for hypersonic glide vehicles provides a concrete technical foundation.
- The topic aligns directly with NSWC PHD interests in hypersonic development, guidance, navigation and control, computational modeling, and validation techniques.
- The concept can be framed as applied research that advances online guidance algorithms for dynamic threat avoidance while preserving terminal accuracy.
- The proposal can be expanded into phased research tasks covering algorithm extension, high-fidelity simulation, robustness analysis, and transition-oriented validation.
