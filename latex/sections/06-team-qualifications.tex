\section{Team Qualifications}

California State University, Long Beach, Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering, provides an academic research environment suited to hypersonic guidance research, numerical simulation, flight mechanics, and aerospace systems analysis.

This proposal builds on prior work by David D. Lee on predictive lateral evasive maneuver guidance for hypersonic glide vehicles and model-based numerical predictor-corrector guidance. Additional details on the principal investigator, key personnel, student researchers, facilities, and relevant prior publications will be added in the next draft.
