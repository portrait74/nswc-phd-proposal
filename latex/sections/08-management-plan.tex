\section{Management Plan}

\subsection{Personnel Management Plan}

The project will be managed by the Principal Investigator at California State University, Long Beach, Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering. The PI will be responsible for overall technical direction, sponsor coordination, research task execution, student supervision, quality control of technical outputs, and timely submission of required reports.

The research team is expected to include graduate and undergraduate student researchers supporting trajectory simulation, multi-model NPCG dataset generation, reinforcement learning formulation, software implementation, performance evaluation, and documentation. Student tasks will be assigned according to technical maturity, required background, and project schedule needs. The PI will conduct regular technical meetings to review progress, identify issues, and coordinate upcoming milestones.

Personnel management will emphasize clear task ownership, documented technical assumptions, reproducible analysis workflows, and frequent internal review of simulation results. Key personnel responsibilities will be aligned with the Statement of Work tasks, and progress will be tracked against milestone objectives and deliverable schedules.

\subsection{Data Management Plan}

The project will generate simulation data, model configuration files, source code, trained reinforcement learning policy artifacts, analysis scripts, figures, and technical reports. Data management will focus on traceability, reproducibility, access control, and appropriate handling of any sponsor-provided or export-controlled information.

Project data will be organized by task, scenario, model configuration, and simulation campaign. Each dataset will include sufficient metadata to identify the vehicle model, atmospheric and aerodynamic assumptions, threat scenario, guidance configuration, random seed or dispersion settings, and evaluation metrics. Version control will be used for proposal source files, analysis scripts, non-sensitive documentation, and software components suitable for repository management.

Any government-furnished information, controlled unclassified information, export-controlled technical data, or sponsor-restricted material will be handled in accordance with applicable sponsor and institutional requirements. Such material will not be placed in public repositories. Publicly shareable materials will be separated from restricted materials, and any release of data, software, or technical reports will be reviewed for compliance with applicable publication, data rights, export control, and award requirements.

At the completion of the project, the team will provide the technical reports and other agreed deliverables specified in the award. The final data package, if required, will be organized to support sponsor review of the methods, assumptions, performance results, and transition recommendations.
