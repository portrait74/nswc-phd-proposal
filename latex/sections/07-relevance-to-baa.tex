\section{Relevance to NSWC PHD BAA}

The proposed research aligns with Topic 2, Hypersonic \& Suborbital Research and Validation, by advancing guidance, navigation, and control methods optimized for hypersonic flight. The effort also supports computational modeling and validation of advanced hypersonic technologies through high-fidelity simulation, robustness analysis, and transition-oriented assessment.

The proposed framework addresses a dynamic threat-avoidance problem that is not fully captured by conventional static no-fly-zone guidance methods. By combining physics-based multi-model NPCG with reinforcement learning, the research aims to improve robustness to model uncertainty and reduce real-time computational burden while maintaining mission-relevant targeting and threat-avoidance performance.
