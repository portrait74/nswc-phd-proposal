\section{Budget Justification}

\subsection{Senior Personnel}

Senior personnel effort will support technical leadership, research direction, guidance algorithm development, simulation design, student supervision, sponsor coordination, and preparation of technical deliverables.

\subsection{Student Researchers}

Graduate and undergraduate student researchers may support simulation implementation, multi-model NPCG dataset generation, reinforcement learning training, performance analysis, documentation, and preparation of research results. Student participation will be aligned with the technical tasks and schedule in Volume I.

\subsection{Fringe Benefits}

Fringe benefits will be calculated in accordance with applicable CSULB and California State University policies and rates.

\subsection{Facilities and Administrative Costs}

Facilities and administrative costs will be applied in accordance with the applicable CSULB negotiated indirect cost rate or other sponsor-approved rate.
